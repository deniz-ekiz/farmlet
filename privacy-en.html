Farmlet Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026
Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how the Farmlet Android app ("Farmlet", "the app") handles data. In short: your study data (sessions, subjects, goals, stats) stays only on your device; Farmlet does not collect it or send it anywhere. The app is free; there are no ads and no account. The one exception is the optional Farmlet Pro purchase: it is made through Google Play, and your subscription status is processed by our subscription infrastructure provider RevenueCat (see "Farmlet Pro purchases" below).

Who we are

Farmlet is developed by Anarres Apps. For any privacy question, contact: contact@denizekiz.com

Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following information only in its private storage on your device:

  • Focus sessions: start time, planned length, pause information, mode (Flexible / Strict), outcome (completed or withered), and the subject and crop the session was for.
  • Subjects, fields and barn: the subjects you add (their names), the type and growth stage of the crop in each field, the crops you have harvested and their harvest dates.
  • Goals and countdowns: the goals you set and the names and dates of your countdowns.
  • Achievements and animals: the achievements you earned and the animals you unlocked.
  • Break: if a break is running, its start and length (deleted when the break ends).
  • Stats and streak: calculated on your device from those records; never sent anywhere.
  • Farmlet Pro status: whether Pro is on (the last known status, so the app works correctly without internet).
  • Settings: your language, theme, keep-screen-on and dimming, vibration and sound (ambient sound, effects) preferences; your character choice (girl / boy) and the name you gave your character; if you turned on "Allowed apps", the package names of the apps you picked.

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Note: if Android backup is turned on for your device, the operating system may back up app data to your Google account under its own rules. That backup lives in your account and under Google's control; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your Android settings.

With Farmlet Pro you can back up your data to a file (Settings > Backup). This is a file backup that you start yourself: the app writes the file only to the place you choose, or reads it from the file you choose; it does not send the file anywhere and makes no network request for this. Where you save the file (for example Google Drive) is governed by that service's own rules. The file does not contain your purchase information.

Data we do not collect

Farmlet does not collect, record or share with third parties (for Farmlet Pro purchases see the section below):

  • Your name, email, phone number or any account information (there is no account)
  • Your location
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • Advertising ID or device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports

The app has no ads, no analytics tools and no server that receives your study data. It does not need an internet connection to work. The internet permission is used only for Farmlet Pro purchases and for checking the subscription status (Google Play and RevenueCat); your sessions, subjects, goals and other study data are never sent. (Google Play may show crash reports to developers through its own channel if the device's "share usage and diagnostics data" setting is on; that is Google's own mechanism, not the app's.)

Farmlet Pro purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Farmlet Pro is optional. When you buy it, or when the app checks your subscription status:

  • Payment is made through Google Play. Google processes your card and payment details; we never see them. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., subscription infrastructure; our service provider) processes the following to verify whether your subscription is active: purchase history (which Farmlet Pro product you bought and when, subscription and renewal status, the Google Play purchase token), a random anonymous app user ID generated by the app (not linked to your name, email or advertising ID) and your country / currency and language to show prices. RevenueCat uses this only on our behalf to process purchases and manage subscriptions; data is encrypted in transit. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Your study data (sessions, subjects, goals, countdowns, stats, your character's name and the like) is never sent.
  • Even if you never use Farmlet Pro, the app may contact RevenueCat to check the subscription status; in that case only the anonymous ID and "no purchase" are processed.
  • If you want your purchase record deleted, write to contact@denizekiz.com; we will delete the record tied to the anonymous ID from RevenueCat. (Google Play's own purchase records are under Google's control.)

Permissions and why we ask for them

PermissionWhy
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)For the countdown notification during a session, the notification when a session or break ends, the warning when you leave the app, and (if you turn it on) the evening goal reminder. If you decline, the app still works; you just get no notifications.
Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)To show the end-of-session and end-of-break notifications right on time. Optional; without it the notification arrives at an approximate time.
Keep screen on / wake lock (WAKE_LOCK)So the screen does not turn off by itself during a session (you can turn this off in settings), and so the device does not fall asleep while ambient sound plays on a locked screen.
Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK)Only if you turn ambient sound on: a media playback service shown with a notification keeps the sound from being cut when the screen is locked or the app is in the background. It stops when ambient sound stops or when you swipe the app away from recents.
Vibration (VIBRATE)For vibration when a session ends, a crop is harvested or a crop withers. You can turn this off in settings.
Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)To restore scheduled notifications if your phone restarts.
Internet (INTERNET)Only for Farmlet Pro purchases and checking the subscription status (Google Play, RevenueCat). Your study data is not sent.
Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING)So you can buy Farmlet Pro through Google Play.
Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)A standard permission of the audio and purchase components (to check whether a connection exists). Nothing is sent with this permission.
Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)Only if you turn on the optional "Allowed apps" feature. So your crop does not wither when you switch to apps you picked (like a dictionary), when you come back to the session the app checks on your device which app was in the foreground during that time and whether the screen was on or locked. You grant this in Android settings and can revoke it at any time.
Do Not Disturb access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY)Only if you turn on the optional "Turn on Do Not Disturb during sessions" setting. It turns your phone's Do Not Disturb mode on while a session runs and puts it back when the session ends. It does not read the content of your notifications. You grant this access in Android settings and can revoke it at any time.

During a session, the app checks on the device whether the screen is on or locked. This is only used to decide whether a session should wither; it is not recorded and never leaves your device. While "Dim screen during sessions" is on, the app only temporarily lowers the brightness of its own window; it does not touch Android's system brightness setting.

Allowed apps and usage access: If you turn this feature on, the package names of the apps you picked (for example, the name of a dictionary app) and the total time spent in allowed apps during each session are stored in the app storage on your device. When you come back to a session, Android usage events (which app came to the foreground, when the screen turned on or off) are read on your device for the session period only, to decide whether your crop withers. These events are not recorded, are not used for anything else and never leave your device. The list of launcher apps on your phone is read only to show the picker; it is not stored or sent. The feature is entirely optional; if you do not grant the permission or pick any apps, everything else in the app works.

Ambient sound: The forest, rain indoors, ocean waves and calm tones sounds are audio files bundled inside the app; nothing is downloaded and the microphone is not used. Your chosen type and volume are stored on your device as settings.

Children and students

Farmlet is made for students preparing for exams, and some of them may be under 18. The app collects no personal data such as names or email addresses from anyone, of any age; for a Farmlet Pro purchase only the purchase information described above is processed. If you are under 18, talk to a parent before buying; Google Play family settings can require approval for purchases. The app is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, write to contact@denizekiz.com.

Sharing of data

We do not sell or rent any data that could identify you. Farmlet Pro purchase information is processed only by our service providers (Google Play, RevenueCat) to complete purchases and verify subscriptions; it is not shared for advertising or any other purpose.

Your rights

Since your study data lives only on your device, it is entirely under your control: you can delete everything at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. If you want your Farmlet Pro purchase record (RevenueCat) deleted, or have any other question, write to contact@denizekiz.com.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above. For a significant change (for example, new data processing being introduced), we will also announce it inside the app.
